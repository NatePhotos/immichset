# Your name, address and logo

These appear on every gallery cover and in the footer of every email. You can skip them during setup and add them any time:
**Connections › Your business** in the admin.

## Name and address

- **Business name:** shown on gallery covers and as the sender's name in emails.
- **Postal address (or PO box):** printed in the footer of every email you send. The law in many places requires it, so
  immichset won't send a real mailing until it's set.
- **Website and social link:** shown in email footers.

## Your logo

On the same screen, **Logo › Upload a logo**.

What to upload:
- A **PNG** with a **transparent background**.
- **Black artwork** (the page colours it white on dark backgrounds for you).
- Around **512 by 512 pixels** is plenty. Under 2 MB.

If your logo is a JPEG or a PNG with a white box behind it, it would show up as a solid block. immichset checks for that and
tells you. Export it again from your design app with the background turned off ("transparent"), or use a free tool such as
remove.bg or your phone's photo editor to cut the background out.

You'll see a preview on a light and a dark background. **Use the default** puts the plain aperture mark back.
Browsers remember the old logo for a few minutes, so a change may not show immediately.

The logo is stored in the data folder, so it survives updates.
